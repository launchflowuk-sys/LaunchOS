What we need from you

Connecting your social accounts

So we can write, schedule and publish your posts, we need permission to post to your Facebook Page and your Instagram. This is how you give it — about ten minutes, no passwords, and reversible at any time.

The short version
  1. Add LaunchFlow as a Partner on your Facebook Page.
  2. Make sure your Instagram is a Business or Creator account, and linked to that Page.
  3. Tell us your Page name. We find everything else ourselves.

What we will never ask you for

  • Your Facebook or Instagram password. Ever, for any reason.
  • Access to your personal profile. We only ever reach the business Page you grant.
  • A login code from your phone.

Everything below is granted through Meta's own Partner system, which means you hold the switch. You can remove our access from the same screen in under a minute, whenever you like, and you do not need to tell us first.

Before you start

Three things make this straightforward. If any one of them isn't true, tell us and we'll sort it with you on a call.

You're a Page admin
You need admin rights on your own Facebook Page. If someone else set it up, they'll need to do this or make you an admin first.
A Meta Business portfolio
Most businesses already have one. If you don't, there's an alternative route in step one that skips it.
Ten minutes, on a computer
It can be done on a phone, but the Business Suite settings screens are much easier on a desktop browser.
Four steps

How to do it

  1. Add LaunchFlow as a Partner on your Page

    In Meta Business Suite, open your business settings and follow this path:

    Business Suite Settings› Partners› Add Partner

    It asks for our business ID. This is it:

    LaunchFlow business ID 925233161834616

    Then tick your Page and give Content access — sometimes shown as Manage Page. That permission lets us create and publish posts. It does not let us delete your Page, change who owns it, or spend money.

    No Business portfolio? Then skip this screen and simply add hello@launchflow.co.uk as an admin on your Page instead. Partner access is tidier — it survives your staff changing and puts no individual in the middle — so it's worth setting up a portfolio eventually, but it is not a blocker today.

  2. Check your Instagram is a Business or Creator account

    In the Instagram app on your phone:

    Instagram app Settings and privacy› Account type and tools› Switch to professional account
    This step isn't optional, and it isn't our rule. Meta does not allow any software — ours or anyone else's — to publish to a personal Instagram account. There is no workaround. Switching is free, takes about thirty seconds, keeps all your posts and followers, and you can switch back whenever you want.

  3. Link that Instagram account to your Facebook Page

    Back in Business Suite:

    Business Suite Settings› Instagram accounts› Connect

    Sign in to Instagram when it asks, and connect it to the same Page from step one. Once they're linked we read the Instagram details off the Page automatically — you never have to hunt down an account ID or send us anything technical.

  4. Tell us your Page name

    Reply to us with the name of the Page exactly as it appears on Facebook, and we'll do the rest. If anything above didn't look the way it's described here — Meta moves these menus around more often than anyone would like — send us a screenshot and we'll point at the right button.

What happens next

Once access is in place, nothing is published without you seeing it first.

We draft, you approve
Every post appears in your LaunchFlow portal before it goes anywhere. You approve, edit or reject it. Nothing publishes on its own.
You set the boundaries
Tell us what you never want said — no discount language, no claims about results, no photos of customers — and it's written into your content brief.
You keep ownership
The accounts stay yours throughout. We're a partner on them, not an owner of them, and removing us changes nothing about your Page or your followers.

Worth knowing

A few things about how these platforms actually behave, so nothing comes as a surprise later.

Instagram captions are final
Once an Instagram post is published its caption cannot be edited by anybody, including you. Facebook's can. That's why we ask you to read Instagram drafts properly rather than skim them.
Fifty Instagram posts a day
Meta's limit, per account, per 24 hours. Comfortable for a content plan; worth knowing if we ever bulk-load a back catalogue for you.
Removing access stops posting silently
If you tidy up your Partners list and remove us, scheduled posts simply stop with no warning to either of us. A quick message if you're reorganising saves a confusing week.
Google Business Profile is optional
If you'd like us posting your offers and updates there too, add us as a Manager — never Owner. Manager is enough to post and leaves you in full control.
