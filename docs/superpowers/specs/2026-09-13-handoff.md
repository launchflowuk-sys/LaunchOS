### The landing page

A **separate nginx container**, not a Next route. The page is an approved
design and the bytes are the specification — porting 39 lines of dense HTML to
JSX and scoping its global CSS away from the live marketing site was risk for
no gain, and nothing on the page needs the database or `@launchos/core`.

All eight source files match `SOURCE-SHA256.json` byte for byte except
`index.html`, which differs **only in its `<head>`**: a self-canonical,
`noindex, follow`, the Open Graph tags reusing the site's existing approved
share card, and the `tracking.js` include. No section, word, colour, class,
spacing or image changed. `apps/grow/reference/index.html` is the untouched
copy to diff against.

Verified locally in the container: all seven CTAs carry the full campaign
string including `fbclid` and `gclid`; three workflow tabs with arrow-key
navigation; six FAQ accordions; all five images at their manifest dimensions;
no horizontal overflow at 360, 390, 768 or 1440; the mobile sticky bar appears
below 768 and keeps its safe-area padding; a missing file is a 404, never the
page.

### Attribution was broken in three places

Forwarding UTM tags to `/start` would have proved nothing. None of this worked:

1. **`safeSource` allowed five UTM tags and nothing else.** Paid clicks very
   often carry no UTM at all — auto-tagging sends `gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`
   or `fbclid` instead — so a paid click was recorded as a direct visit.
2. **Nothing copied the draft's source onto the lead.** `captureLeadFromDraft`
   never touched `metadata.attribution`, which is what the leads board's
   Campaign column, the `utmCampaign` filter and `costPerLeadByCampaign` all
   read. **Every lead the brief funnel has ever created shows a blank there.**
3. **A returning visitor's campaign was dropped.** Source is only read in
   `POST /session`, which does not run when the thirty-day cookie already names
   a draft. A second ad click was invisible.

First touch lives in `metadata.attribution` and is never overwritten; latest
touch sits beside it in `metadata.attributionLatest`.

**Historic leads are not backfilled.** The tags were never recorded, so there
is nothing to backfill from — only leads created from now on carry a campaign.

### Measurement, dormant

There was **no analytics of any kind** in this codebase before today — no
gtag, no GTM, no pixel, no consent banner. What was added loads nothing until
real ids exist.

The primary conversion is **a brief the backend has confirmed**, reported in
one place: `funnel.tsx`, after `/submit` returns a reference. Early contact
capture is unchanged and still happens — it is simply not what the ad platforms
are told to buy. A CTA click is `funnel_cta_click`, explicitly not a lead.

Ids are read on the server and passed to the client as props, **not**
`NEXT_PUBLIC_`, so changing a pixel id is a Coolify variable and a restart
rather than a rebuild.

## 3. Decisions Shoji made today — do not re-ask

- **Landing page is `noindex, follow`.** It says most of what the homepage says
  and a second copy competing for the same terms costs more than it wins.
- **Costing is ex-VAT, with the gross shown alongside.** So every stored amount
  needs its VAT treatment recorded rather than inferred — a Hetzner invoice
  under reverse charge and a Hostinger one with UK VAT cannot be told apart
  from the number alone.
- **Reconcile against the real provider bills: yes.** Build it so reconciliation
  switches on per provider; a missing key means "not reconciled", never a zero.
- **Cost register first**, ahead of the usage ledger — which displaces the
  admin email setting and the WhatsApp alerts rather than cancelling them.

## 4. The cost register — built 13 Sep

Done and testable locally. Full write-up in `docs/COSTING.md`.

- `supplier_costs` **generalised** rather than duplicated: `source`,
  `business`, `vat_treatment`, `notes`, and `external_id` now nullable. One
  table, so a cost cannot be double-counted by existing in two.
- New `fx_rates`: one rate per day per pair. A cost converts at the rate for
  the first of the month reported, so **a closed month never changes**. A
  missing rate excludes the row and names the currency — it is never 1.0.
- **Money → Profit**: revenue collected against register cost, ex-VAT with
  gross alongside, cost split by business, annualised.
- **Settings → Costs** gained the register: add, edit, delete, prefill from
  `KNOWN_SUPPLIERS` (13 providers the code already talks to, priced at zero),
  and a per-currency rate field for whatever cannot convert.
- **Anthropic CSV reader.** Admin keys are Team/Enterprise only, so the Console
  export is the only route. `api_key` turned out to be the business
  attribution for free — every business already has its own key.
- `profitReport` returns `complete: false` and the list of what is missing, so
  no screen can present a margin that silently omits the variable cost.

Verified end to end in the browser: prefill seeded 13 rows, USD and EUR rates
saved, Hetzner priced at €24 → £20.64, total £47.39, margin £1,287.00 −
£47.39 = £1,239.61, and the reader split a sample export by business and model.

## 5. Traps

- **`core.autocrlf=true` on this Windows checkout.** `.gitattributes` now pins
  `*.sh` to LF and `Dockerfile.grow` strips `\r` anyway. Without both, a
  `git stash pop` hands docker a CRLF entrypoint and the container dies with
  `no such file or directory` — naming the script, not the line endings.
- **`packages/core/src/projects/project-update.test.ts` fails on a clean
  tree.** "the week the reporter reads" expects `['The homepage design']` and
  gets `[]`. Confirmed pre-existing by stashing all of today's work and
  re-running. It looks date-dependent and the 11 Sep handoff recorded a fully
  green suite, so something about the current date broke it. **Not
  investigated.**
- **`heartbeat.test.ts` is genuinely flaky under load** — it failed in one full
  run, passed alone, and passed in the next. The machine was out of RAM at the
  time (0.1GB free of 31.6), which also killed a whole vitest run with
  `ERR_IPC_CHANNEL_CLOSED`. Diagnose memory before blaming a test.
- **Consent is per origin.** A visitor who accepts on `grow.launchflow.co.uk`
  is asked again on `launchflow.co.uk`. Deliberately not worked around with a
  shared-domain cookie.
